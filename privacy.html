What we keep, and what we never touch

Most of NUMIO never leaves your phone. This page says exactly what does, why, and how to delete it. It is written to be read, not to be survived.

Last updated 25 August 2026 · numerology.lifestyle

Who we are

NUMIO is operated by The Solar Consultant. For anything on this page, including any request to see or delete your data, write to support@thesolarconsultant.uk. We are the data controller for the information described here.

Your profile stays on your phone

Your date of birth, your name, everyone you add to your family space and every reading built from them are stored in your own browser, on your own device. They are not uploaded, not backed up by us, and not visible to us.

Three things are kept there:

WhatContains
Your profileYour name, date of birth, the numbers calculated from them, how you answered the optional question about yourself, and each family member you have added
Usage logThe last 300 things you did in the app — which screens, in what order. Never leaves the device
A dismissal noteWhether you closed the "add to home screen" prompt, so it stops asking

Clearing your browser data, or using Clear all my data in the app, erases all of it immediately and permanently. We cannot recover it, because we never had it.

NUMIO sets no cookies at all. Not for analytics, not for advertising, not for anything.

What does reach our server

Two things, and they are deliberately kept apart.

1. Anonymous counts — from everyone

So we know how many people finish a reading rather than only how many volunteer to tell us, the app records four moments: checkout started, paid, profile finished, reading opened.

Each record holds only the name of that moment, which plan it was, your Life Path root (one of nine possible values), an age band (one of six), how you answered the optional “how do you describe yourself” question (man, woman, or prefer not to say), and whether you were using the app or the website. No name, no date of birth, no email, no device identifier, no advertising ID, and no cookie. We do not log your IP address against it. None of it can be traced back to a person, including by us.

2. Your calculated profile — only if you tick the box

At the end of building your reading there is an unticked box offering to add your profile to our research set, so we can see which patterns are common across everyone. If you tick it, we store your name, your date of birth, the numbers calculated from them, your dominant themes, your age band, that same optional answer about how you describe yourself, and which plan you are on.

If you do not tick it, none of that is ever sent. The box is never pre-ticked.

Family members are never included, even if you opt in. Only your own profile is sent. Anyone else you have added — partner, children, parents — stays on your device only.

That endpoint has no field for an email address or phone number and rejects any attempt to add one, so it cannot quietly become a marketing list.

The one question about you, not your numbers

On the step where you enter your name we ask, optionally, whether you would describe yourself as a man, a woman, or would prefer not to say. You can simply not answer it, and nothing in the app treats you differently either way — it changes no part of your reading.

It exists for one reason: so we can see who NUMIO is actually reaching. In the anonymous counts it sits alongside the age band, as one of three possible answers next to one of six — neither narrows to a person, and there is no name or date of birth in that record to attach it to. In the research set it is stored with the rest of your profile, and only if you ticked the box.

We do not use it to target you, and we do not sell it. Changing or removing your answer is the same as everything else on your device: open the app and change it, or clear your data.

Changing your mind

If you opted in and want it gone, open your dashboard and use Stop sharing my profile for research & erase it. That deletes the record from our database straight away. Your own reading on your device is untouched.

You can also email us and we will do it for you. You do not need an account, and you do not need to explain why.

Why we are allowed to hold it

  • Your profile in the research set: your consent, given by ticking that box. You can withdraw it at any time, as above.
  • The anonymous counts: our legitimate interest in knowing whether the product works. They contain no personal data, so they do not identify you.
  • Payment records: necessary to perform the contract when you buy a reading, and to meet the tax and accounting rules we are subject to.

How long we keep it

  • On your device: until you delete it. There is no expiry and no automatic upload.
  • Shared profiles: until you withdraw. There is no other trigger for deletion, so if you want it gone, tell us.
  • Anonymous counts: kept indefinitely, because they hold nothing personal to expire.

Who else is involved

  • Vercel — hosts the site and runs the small amount of server code. Like every web host it processes the technical details of a request, including your IP address, in order to serve the page.
  • Prisma — provides the database the two records above are stored in.
  • Google Fonts — the two typefaces are served by Google, so loading a page tells Google your IP address and browser. This is the only third party the page itself contacts.
  • Stripe — will handle payments when checkout goes live. Card details go directly to Stripe and are never seen or stored by us.

There are no advertising or analytics trackers on this site — no Google Analytics, no Meta pixel, no TikTok pixel.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask us for a copy of what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, ask us to delete it, or object to how we use it. Email support@thesolarconsultant.uk and we will respond within one month.

In practice, for most people the answer is short: everything is on your phone, and you can delete it yourself in two taps.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled it, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Children

NUMIO is meant for adults. You can add children to your family space, and their names and dates of birth stay on your device exactly like everyone else's — they are never sent to us, even if you opt in to sharing your own profile. Please only add a child if you are their parent or guardian.

What NUMIO is not

NUMIO is an interpretive tool for reflection. It is not medical, legal, psychological or financial advice, and nothing it produces is a prediction. Please do not make decisions that matter on the strength of it alone.

Changes

If we change what we collect, we will change this page and update the date at the top. If the change is significant, we will say so in the app rather than leave you to notice.
